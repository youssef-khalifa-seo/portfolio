Competitor Backlink Analysis — Evaluation Criteria

Each Referring Domain in the competitor backlink profiles was evaluated against a structured set of criteria to determine quality and strategic value — filtering out low-value links and identifying high-priority targets for outreach.

Domain Authority (DA ≥ 30)
Referring Domain Traffic Volume
Anchor Text & Target Page
Link Placement in Content
Topical Relevance to Niche
Number of Referring Domains per Competitor
Backlink Type (Contextual · Guest Post · Directory · etc.)
Analysis Process
01
Competitor Identification & Backlink Extraction
Identified the top competitors in the UAE company formation space and extracted their full backlink profiles using Ahrefs — focusing on Referring Domains rather than raw link counts to assess the true authority spread.
02
Referring Domain Quality Filtering
Filtered all Referring Domains by DA (minimum 30), traffic levels, and topical relevance to the company formation niche — eliminating spam domains and retaining only authoritative, relevant sources worth targeting.
03
Anchor Text & Link Context Analysis
Mapped the anchor text used for each backlink and identified the target pages receiving those links — then assessed whether the link appeared contextually within body content, in a directory listing, footer, or navigation, to evaluate its true SEO value.
04
Backlink Type Classification
Classified every backlink by type — contextual editorial, guest post, business directory, news mention, forum, or resource page — to understand which link-building tactics competitors are leveraging and which are most replicable.
05
Competitive Strategy & Outreach Plan
Built a prioritized outreach strategy targeting the same high-quality Referring Domains competitors are using — with keyword recommendations for anchor text to compete on the most valuable search terms in the niche.
Key Findings
🟠 Observation
Contextual Links Dominate
Top-ranking competitors earn the majority of their valuable backlinks from contextual placements within relevant articles — not directories or footers — making content-driven outreach the highest-priority tactic.
🟠 Observation
Anchor Text Concentration
Competitors concentrate anchor text on commercial keywords like "تأسيس شركة في دبي" and "فري زون الإمارات" — creating clear anchor text targets for a competing link-building campaign.
🟡 Opportunity
Shared Referring Domains
Several high-DA Referring Domains link to multiple competitors — indicating they regularly accept outreach and are accessible targets for a new link-building campaign.
🟡 Opportunity
Untapped Relevant Domains
Identified high-DA business and legal resource websites with strong topical relevance to company formation that no competitor has secured links from yet — first-mover outreach opportunity.
Strategic Insight

The fastest path to link-building ROI is targeting the shared Referring Domains that already link to multiple competitors — these sites are pre-qualified as willing to link in this niche, reducing outreach friction significantly.
